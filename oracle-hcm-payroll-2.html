1. Oracle Payroll Basics (24 answers)
What is Oracle HCM Payroll?
Complete global payroll management engine integrated with HCM Cloud; handles calculations, taxation, compliance, payments, reporting.

Explain the payroll processing lifecycle.
Pre-payroll → Payroll Run → Prepayment → Costing → Payment Distribution → Archive → GL Transfer → Reporting.
Difference between Payroll Relationship and Work Relationship?
Work Relationship = Employment relationship between worker and legal employer.
Payroll Relationship = Payroll processing relationship tied to a Payroll Statutory Unit (PSU).
A worker can have multiple work relationships, multiple assignments, and usually one payroll relationship per PSU.
What is a Payroll Statutory Unit (PSU)?
Legal entity responsible for tax/statutory reporting; groups legal employers under same legislation.
What is a Legislative Data Group (LDG)?
Top-level grouping for a country/legislation, holding statutory rules, currencies, and legal reporting units.
What is Payroll Definition?
Defines processing rules, consolidation group, currency, payment method, and valid calendars for a payroll.
What is Payroll Frequency?
Schedule type: Monthly, Bi-Weekly, Weekly, Semi-Monthly, determines pay period recurrence.
Difference between Monthly and Bi-Weekly payroll?
Monthly: 12 periods/year, Bi-Weekly: 26 periods/year (every 2 weeks). Bi-weekly often used in US/Canada.
What are Payroll Elements?
Reusable components representing earnings (salary, bonus) or deductions (tax, insurance).
Difference between Earnings and Deductions elements?
Earnings increase net pay; Deductions decrease net pay.
What are Standard and Supplemental Earnings?
Standard: recurring regular pay; Supplemental: one-off ad-hoc payments like bonus, commission.
What are Voluntary and Involuntary deductions?
Voluntary: employee elected (charity, retirement extra); Involuntary: legal (garnishment, child support).
What is Element Classification?
Categorizes element behavior (Earnings, Deduction, Employer Contribution, Absence, Information).
What is an Input Value?
User-defined parameter (amount, hours, rate) passed to element for payroll calculation.
What is an Element Eligibility?
Defines which employees receive an element using eligibility profiles or fast formulas.
What are Balance Dimensions?
Time context: period-to-date, quarter-to-date, year-to-date, assignment, tax reporting dimension.
What are Payroll Balances?
Accumulated values from element entries, e.g., Gross Earnings YTD, Total Tax QTD.
What is Cost Allocation?
Distribution of payroll expenses to GL accounts, departments, projects via cost allocation flexfield.
What is Partial Period Accrual?
Primarily used in Absence Management to calculate accruals for partial enrollment periods (e.g., employee enrolls mid-month, accrual is pro-rated).
Retroactive Pay setup components?
Retro event triggers, retro elements, formula for recalculation, notification settings.
Proration definition?
Adjusting salary/allowance based on days worked during partial period (hire, termination, mid-month change).
Garnishment element?
Involuntary deduction element with priority, limits and court-ordered amounts.

3. Payroll Processing (Corrected: QuickPay, Rollback/Reversal, Assignment)
What are the steps in payroll processing?
Submit payroll → Calculate → Prepayments → Costing → Payment Distribution → Archive → Transfer to GL.
Explain QuickPay. CORRECTED
QuickPay processes payroll for a single employee outside the normal payroll run. It can also produce actual payroll results and payments if completed through all payroll actions (prepayment, costing, archive).
What is a Payroll Run?
Main calculation process generating earnings/deductions and balances.
What is a Prepayment process?
Creates payment records, net pay, and calculates payment distribution.
What is Costing?
Allocation of payroll costs to GL accounts (cost centers, projects).
What is Payment Distribution?
Determines how net pay is delivered per payment method (bank, cheque).
What is Payroll Archive?
Stores finalized payroll results for reporting and audit.
What is Payroll Interface?
Transfer data to/from external systems (GL, third-party payrolls).
What is a Payslip?
Employee earnings & deductions statement, generated from archive.
How do you retry a failed payroll process?
Navigate to Payroll Flow → View errors → correct data → Retry action.
What is Payroll Relationship Action?
Internal record capturing each payroll action for an employee (run, reversal).
What is Payroll Assignment? CORRECTED
A Payroll Assignment links an employee assignment to a payroll relationship and enables payroll processing for that assignment. It is the bridge between work relationship and payroll data.
What are Payroll Run Results?
Generated element entries per employee for a payroll period.
Explain Payroll Reversal. CORRECTED
Creates negative entries to offset already processed payroll results after payment or completion (full audit trail).
What is Rollback in Payroll? CORRECTED
Rollback removes payroll process results before they are finalized or archived (undoes incomplete run).
Difference between Retry, Rollback, and Reversal? CORRECTED
Retry: re-run failed step; Rollback: removes incomplete results before payment; Reversal: negative transaction after payment completed.
What is Payroll Validation?
Pre-run validation of eligibility, element entries, costing, absence.
How do you process Off-Cycle Payroll?
Unschedule payment (bonus, termination) using off-cycle payroll definition.
Explain Supplemental Payroll.
One-time additional payments separate from regular payroll.
What are Payroll Messages?

How to process final settlement?
Termination off-cycle payroll including last salary, EOSB, leave encashment.
Duplicated payment recovery?
Recovery element entry or negative earnings in next payroll.

10. Payroll Functional Scenarios
Employee salary changed mid-month. Calculation?
Pro-rated salary: (old salary * days_old) + (new salary * days_new) / total month days.
Retroactive salary increases handling?
Update effective-dated salary element, retro event triggers recalculation for previous periods.
Employee joined in middle of payroll cycle?
Proration of salary based on working days, start date element entry from hire date.
Employee resigned before month-end settlement?
Run termination off-cycle, prorate last month salary, EOSB, leave encashment, airfare.
Unpaid leave deductions?
Unpaid absence element reduces salary proportionally.
Duplicate payment recovery method?
Recovery element entry (negative) or payroll reversal and recalc.
Payroll completed with incorrect balance fix?
Adjust balance via balance adjustment element / retro or reversal.
Bonus for selected employees?
Create supplemental bonus element, assign via eligibility profile or batch element entry.
Multiple assignments payroll processing?
Payroll relationship groups multiple assignments, allocate earnings split or primary assignment.
Troubleshoot missing element entries?
Check element eligibility, effective dates, payroll relationship status, link to payroll.
Payroll costing failed – checks?
Validate cost allocation, KFF structure, suspense account, element override.
Payslip not generated troubleshooting?
Check archive completion, payslip template, person’s assignment status.
Employee payment method missing scenario?
System falls back to organization payment method, else error in prepayment.
Bank transfer file rejected? GCC
Check bank account format (IBAN), ensure WPS SIF format (not NACHA), payment method setup for WPS.
Year-end adjustments process?
Balance adjustments, off-cycle payroll, update tax balances.

🌍 GCC Payroll (UPDATED: GOSI, GPSSA, Gratuity Decree 33, WPS, MOL)
What is GCC Payroll? GCC
Payroll processing for Gulf countries (UAE, KSA, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain) with EOSB, social insurance, housing/transport allowance, WPS.
Which countries are included? GCC
UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain.
Major payroll components in GCC?
Basic, Housing Allowance, Transport, Overtime, Leave Salary, Airfare, EOSB Gratuity, Social Insurance (GOSI/GPSSA).
What is gratuity in GCC?
Mandatory end-of-service benefit based on years of service and basic salary.
Explain End of Service Benefits (EOSB) - UPDATED UAE DECREE 33
Under UAE Federal Decree Law No. 33 of 2021: First 5 years = 21 days basic salary/year; Above 5 years = 30 days basic salary/year; Maximum = 2 years total salary. Full entitlement for resignation and termination.
Housing & Transport allowance?
Common as fixed percentage of basic (20-25% housing, 10-15% transport).
What is leave salary? CLARIFIED
Depends on company policy and country legislation: Some use basic salary only; others use gross salary or include fixed allowances.
What is airfare allowance? CORRECTED
Airfare allowance is usually company policy driven and common in GCC employment contracts, not universally mandated by labor law for all employees.
Overtime processing in GCC?
Usually basic wage / working hours * 1.25 (normal) or 1.5 (night/holiday).
Statutory deductions in GCC?
Social insurance (GOSI for KSA, GPSSA for UAE), sometimes dependent on nationality.
Explain GOSI? UPDATED %
Saudi social insurance for nationals: Employee approx 10%, Employer approx 12% (pension + unemployment). Subject to GOSI regulatory updates.
What is GPSSA? UPDATED %
UAE pension for nationals: Employee 5%, Employer 12.5%, Government 2.5% (percentages can vary by emirate).
Explain WPS (Wage Protection System).
UAE electronic salary transfer; employers upload SIF file via banks/MOHRE. NACHA not used.
What is labor card requirement?
Work permit required before payroll enrollment for expats.
MOL reporting for KSA? CLARIFIED
Qiwa platform manages employment contracts and labor compliance. Payroll/GOSI reporting handled separately via GOSI and banking/WPS systems.
Configure gratuity elements (UAE Decree 33)?
Create EOSB element, fast formula: IF service_years <=5 THEN (basic/30)*21*service_years ELSE (basic/30)*21*5 + (basic/30)*30*(service_years-5). Cap at 2 years basic.
Explain EOS accrual setup?
Run monthly accrual element to build liability balance.
Configure leave salary calculation?
Leave encashment element using balance dimension "Leave Accrued" * daily rate (basic or gross per policy).
How do you configure airfare eligibility?
Eligibility condition service > 1 year, annual airfare element (company policy driven).
Explain housing allowance calculations?
% of basic or fixed amount; non-taxable in some GCC.
Configure overtime rules?
Fast formula with day classification (Friday premium, overtime multiplier).
How to configure WPS bank details?
Central bank registration, payment method for WPS SIF format.
Gratuity calculation formula UAE (Decree 33)?
If service 1-5 yrs: 21 days basic salary per year; >5 yrs: 30 days per year extra. Capped: 2 years total basic. Full entitlement for all eligible separations.
Saudi gratuity rules?
Half-month wage per year for first 5 years, full month per year thereafter (based on last basic).
Probation impact on gratuity?
No gratuity if resignation during probation, termination may give partial depending on law.
Resignation vs termination gratuity (UAE Decree 33)?
Under new law (Decree 33 of 2021): Both resignation and termination receive full entitlement (no reduction factors). Old 1/3, 2/3 rules are outdated.
Final settlement processing?
Off-cycle payroll includes last salary + unused leave + gratuity + airfare - recovery.
Multi-country payroll setup GCC?
Separate LDGs per country, localized elements, different payroll definitions.
Localization Fast Formulas used?
EOSB accrual (Decree 33), GOSI brackets, housing allowance calculation.
GCC Payroll compliance?
WPS (UAE), GOSI/GPSSA monthly filing, updated labor law EOSB, Qatar end-of-service.
