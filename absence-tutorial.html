🌴 Oracle HCM Absence Management

Complete Tutorial · Setup Guide · Accrual Formulas · Certification & Interview Q&A
📘 1. What is Oracle Absence Management?
🎯 Definition: Oracle HCM Absence Management is a comprehensive module that automates the tracking, calculation, accrual, and approval of employee time off — including vacation, sick leave, maternity/paternity, unpaid leave, and statutory leaves. It integrates seamlessly with Payroll and Time & Labor.
Key capabilities of Absence Management?
✓ Leave request workflows & approvals
✓ Automatic absence accruals (calendar/service-based)
✓ Balance consumption & carryover rules
✓ Integration with payroll (paid/unpaid absence)
✓ Statutory compliance (FMLA, parental, sick)
Core components of Absence Management?
Absence Plans (leave types), Accrual Formulas (Fast Formula), Absence Types, Certification Requirements, Validation Formulas, Approval Workflows, Balance Definitions.
What is an Absence Plan?
A container defining a leave type: accrual method (periodic or service-based), carryover limits, entitlement cap, payment percentage, and integration with payroll.
Difference between Absence Plan and Absence Type?
Plan is the overarching policy (Vacation Policy). Type is specific leave within plan (Paid Vacation, Unpaid Vacation). One plan can have multiple absence types.
📋 2. Absence Types & Plan Configuration
How to create an Absence Plan?
Setup → Absence Administration → Manage Absence Plans → Define name, classification (Vacation/Sick/Other), accrual method (Periodic Accrual or Service-Based), carryover rules, and maximum balance.
What are absence classifications?
System categories: Vacation, Sick, Bereavement, Maternity/Paternity, Unpaid, Jury Duty, Training, etc. Each classification has default behavior.
Periodic Accrual vs Service-Based Accrual
Periodic: Employees accrue fixed hours per month/quarter (e.g., 8 hrs/month). Service-Based: Accrual rate increases with years of service (e.g., 10 days/year for 0-5 yrs, 15 days after 5 yrs).
Carryover Rules explained.
Define how much unused leave can be carried to next period: maximum carryover (e.g., 10 days), expiration date, and cash-out options.
⚙️ 3. Accrual Fast Formulas (Critical)
📌 Fast Formula use in Absence: Accrual formulas calculate how much leave is earned each period. They can consider service length, grade, full-time equivalency (FTE), and other factors.
/* Sample Fast Formula for Vacation Accrual based on years of service */ Formula Type: Absence Accrual Plan DEFAULT for ASG_FTE_VALUE is 1 DEFAULT for ASG_HIRE_DATE is '1900-01-01' years_of_service = MONTHS_BETWEEN(TRUNC(TODAY()), ASG_HIRE_DATE) / 12 IF years_of_service < 5 THEN monthly_accrual_hours = 6.67 /* 80 hrs per year / 12 */ ELSE IF years_of_service >= 5 AND years_of_service < 10 THEN monthly_accrual_hours = 10 ELSE monthly_accrual_hours = 13.33 END IF /* Multiply by FTE */ result = monthly_accrual_hours * ASG_FTE_VALUE
Common Accrual Database Items (DBIs)
• ASG_HIRE_DATE – employee hire date
• ASG_FTE_VALUE – full-time equivalent
• ASG_WORK_DAY_HOURS – standard daily hours
• ABSENCE_BALANCE_VALUE – current balance
✅ 4. Absence Request Workflow & Approvals
Absence request lifecycle
Employee submits request → Manager approval (or auto-approve) → HR verification (if needed) → Absence recorded → Balance updated → Payroll integration triggered.
How to configure approval rules?
Use BPM Worklist: Define rule conditions (duration>3 days, manager approval; emergency leave → director approval). Use "Manage Absence Approval Rules".
What is a Validation Formula for absence?
Fast formula that checks eligibility before submission: e.g., “Can employee take leave during probation period?” “Maximum consecutive days?”
What is a Certification Type?
Medical certificate or document required for certain absences (sick leave >3 days). Employees can upload documents during request.
⚖️ 5. Absence Balances & Consumption Logic
How are absence balances calculated?
Opening balance (carryover) + Accruals during period - Consumed leave (approved absences) = Current balance. Oracle updates real-time after approval or payroll accrual.
What is "Negative Balance" control?
Plan setting that allows or restricts leave requests when balance insufficient. Options: Hard limit (reject), Soft warning (allow with note).
Balance Initialization for Absence
When implementing, use "Initial Balance Upload" via HDL or Spreadsheet to load historic accrued leaves as opening balances for each employee.
What are Absence Certifications?
Verification milestones: e.g., for long-term sick leave, employee must submit doctor's note every 30 days. System can trigger reminders.
🔄 6. Payroll Integration (Paid & Unpaid Absence)
🔗 Critical Integration: When an absence is approved, Oracle creates corresponding Payroll Elements (Paid Leave earnings or Unpaid Deduction). These elements are picked up during payroll run.
Absence TypePayroll ElementEffect on Pay
Paid VacationVacation Pay (Earnings)Normal salary paid
Sick Leave (Paid)Sick Pay EarningsFull or partial salary
Unpaid LeaveUnpaid DeductionSalary reduced by days missed
Maternity LeaveMaternity BenefitStatutory benefit amount
How is Unpaid Leave deduction calculated?
Formula: (Monthly Salary / Working Days in Month) × Number of Unpaid Days. Oracle generates negative earnings or deduction element automatically.
🏛️ 7. Statutory Leaves (FMLA, Parental, Sick)
FMLA (Family & Medical Leave Act) support
Tracks FMLA entitlement (12 weeks in 12 months), eligibility period, intermittent leave, and certifications. Generates necessary notices and compliance reports.
Maternity/Paternity leave setup
Define plan with statutory weeks, payment percentage (e.g., 100% for 6 weeks, then 60%), and certification required (birth certificate).
How does Oracle handle overlapping statutory leaves?
Rules define which plan takes precedence. For example, FMLA runs concurrent with company parental leave, ensuring total entitlement not exceeded.
📅 8. Absence Patterns & Work Schedules
What are Absence Patterns?
Define recurring absence (e.g., every Friday half-day, weekly therapy). Supports regular, intermittent leave tracking without daily requests.
Work Schedule integration
System uses employee's work schedule (M-F, shift hours) to calculate absence duration in hours or days. Part-time employees accrue prorated leave.

💼 10. Absence Management Interview Q&A
Q1: What is the difference between Absence Plan and Absence Type?
Plan defines policy (accrual, carryover). Type is specific leave within plan (e.g., "Vacation Plan" contains "Paid Vacation", "Unpaid Vacation" types).
Q2: How do you calculate pro-rated accruals for mid-year hires?
Accrual formula can incorporate hire date: annual entitlement × (remaining months/12). Fast Formula can prorate based on hire date within calendar year.
Q3: What happens to absence balances when an employee transfers departments?
Balances are retained unless different leave policies exist. Transfers can map old plan balances to new plan via balance transfer configuration.
Q4: How do you handle unpaid leave deduction in payroll?
Unpaid absence plan links to "Unpaid Deduction" payroll element. During payroll run, system calculates (daily salary × unpaid days) and deducts.
Q5: Explain "Validation Formula" vs "Accrual Formula".
Validation – checks request rules (balance sufficient, notice period). Accrual – calculates how much leave employee earns each period.
Q6: Can an employee have multiple absence plans?
Yes. Employees can be enrolled in vacation, sick, paid time off (PTO), and statutory plans simultaneously. Balances tracked separately.
Q7: How do you configure carryover expiration?
In Absence Plan: Set carryover rule – maximum carryover hours, expiration date (e.g., March 31), and behavior (forfeit or cash-out).
Q8: What is an "Absence Category"?
High-level grouping: Planned vs Unplanned, Paid vs Unpaid, Statutory vs Voluntary. Used for reporting and approval routing.
Q9: How do you test absence accruals without waiting for month-end?
Use "Run Accruals" process in test mode, or run Quick Accrual for a single employee. Check "Absence Accrual Details" report.
Q10: Leave encashment – how is it processed?
At termination or annually, HR runs "Leave Encashment" process that creates one-time earnings element in payroll for unused balance.
Q11: Can managers approve absences in bulk?
Yes, Manager Dashboard includes "Team Absence Requests" where multiple requests can be approved/rejected simultaneously.
Q12: What is an "Absence Half-Day" rule?
Define partial days as .5 day unit. System can split morning/afternoon absence based on work schedule.
Q13: How do you handle retroactive absence approvals?
Manager can approve past-dated request. System adjusts balance from effective date and triggers retro payroll if needed.
Q14: What is a "Holiday Calendar" impact on absence?
If absence includes public holidays, those days may not count against leave balance (configurable per plan).
🔧 11. Common Issues & Troubleshooting
Employee balance not accruing?
Check: 1) Accrual formula active? 2) Enrollment effective date? 3) Run "Calculate Absence Balances" process. 4) FTE value = 0?
Error: "Insufficient balance" but employee has balance
Check leave unit (hours vs days), pending approvals consuming balance, or hard limit on plan. Review "Available Balance" vs "Plan Balance".
Absence request stuck in approval
Check BPM workflow: Is manager assigned? Does manager have role "Line Manager"? Approver may be inactive. Reassign or escalate.
Payroll not deducting unpaid leave
1) Absence plan linked to correct payroll element? 2) Absence approved with effective date in payroll period? 3) Run "Absence to Payroll" extract.
Accrual not prorated for part-time employees
Accrual formula must multiply by ASG_FTE_VALUE (part-time factor). Confirm FTE >0 on assignment.

🧩 13. Real-World Scenario & Solution
Scenario: Company policy: Employees accrue 15 vacation days/year after 1 year of service, prorated monthly. After 5 years, 20 days/year. Max carryover 30 days. Unused leave paid at termination. Need to configure.
Step 1 Create Absence Plan "Vacation" → Classification Vacation
Step 2 Accrual method: Periodic (Monthly)
Step 3 Write Fast Formula: IF years_of_service >=5 THEN monthly = 20/12 ELSE 15/12
Step 4 Carryover rule: Max 30 days
Step 5 Termination rule: Pay out unused balance
Step 6 Link to payroll element "Vacation Pay" for paid absence.
📖 14. Absence Management Glossary
Accrual
Earning leave over time (monthly/annually).
Balance Transfer
Moving leave balance between plans (e.g., acquisition).
Entitlement
Total leave allowed per year.
Intermittent Leave
Leave taken in separate blocks (FMLA).
Certification
Supporting document required.
Negative Balance
When leave taken exceeds available.
