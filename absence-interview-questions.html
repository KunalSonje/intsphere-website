🏆 Oracle HCM Absence Management

🔥 100 INTERVIEW QUESTIONS & ANSWERS – DEEP DIVE 🔥

Covers: accrual formulas, absence plans, balances, payroll integration, fast formulas, validation, statutory leaves (FMLA), OTBI, certification, troubleshooting, real scenarios.

📌 1. Core Concepts & Definitions (1–10)
1. What is Oracle Absence Management and how does it integrate with HCM?
A module that automates leave tracking, accruals, approvals, and balance management. Integrates with Payroll (paid/unpaid deductions), Time & Labor, and Benefits.
2. Explain the difference between an Absence Plan and an Absence Type.
Plan: defines accrual policy, carryover, cap, and payment rules (e.g., Vacation Plan). Type: specific leave under plan (e.g., Paid Vacation, Unpaid Vacation).
3. What are the main absence classifications in Oracle?
Vacation, Sick, Maternity/Paternity, Bereavement, Unpaid, Jury Duty, Training, Other, and Statutory (FMLA, parental leave).
4. What is the purpose of an Absence Calendar?
Defines work patterns, public holidays, and working days; used to compute absence duration (hours/days) and proration.
5. What is a "Certification Type" in absence management?
Required document (medical certificate, death certificate) to validate absence. Can have expiry and re-certification rules.
6. How does Absence Management handle partial days (half-day leaves)?
Via absence patterns or unit of measure configuration (0.5 day). System uses work schedule to determine morning/afternoon.
7. What are the key differences between Periodic Accrual and Service-Based Accrual?
Periodic: fixed amount per month/quarter regardless of service length. Service-based: rate increases with years of service.
8. Describe the absence request lifecycle.
Employee submits → Validation (balance, eligibility) → Manager approval → HR verification (optional) → Balance update → Payroll integration.
9. What is an Absence Balance Definition?
Defines how leave balances are calculated: opening balance + accruals - consumption - carryover adjustments, with optional caps.
10. What is a Negative Balance control and typical settings?
Allows or restricts leave when balance insufficient. Options: Hard limit (reject request), Soft warning (allow with alert).
⚙️ 2. Accruals & Fast Formula (11–20)
11. What Fast Formula types are used in Absence Management?
Accrual formula, Validation formula, Eligibility formula, Rate of Pay formula, and Calculation formula for partial payments.
12. Write a Fast Formula for prorated vacation accrual for mid-year hire.
annual_entitlement = 120; months_rem = 12 - MONTHS_BETWEEN(HIRE_DATE, START_DATE); result = (annual_entitlement/12)*months_rem;
13. Which database items (DBIs) are commonly used in absence accrual formulas?
ASG_HIRE_DATE, ASG_FTE_VALUE, ASG_WORK_DAY_HOURS, ASG_SALARY, ABSENCE_BALANCE_VALUE, ASG_SCHEDULED_HOURS.
14. How do you handle part-time employee accruals?
Multiply accrual rate by ASG_FTE_VALUE (full-time equivalent). Example: monthly_accrual = full_time_accrual * fte_value.
15. Can you use conditional logic in accrual formulas based on job grade?
Yes, use IF ASG_GRADE = 'Manager' THEN accrual_rate = 20 ELSE 12 END IF. Grade data can be fetched from assignment.
16. What is the purpose of "Regular Accrual" process?
Periodic batch process that computes and posts leave accruals to employee balances based on plan definitions and formulas.
17. Explain how to test an accrual formula before deploying.
Use "Test Formula" in Fast Formula editor with sample data, or run Quick Accrual for a single employee and compare results.
18. What is an "Accrual Cap" and where is it defined?
Maximum leave balance an employee can accumulate. Defined in Absence Plan under "Maximum Balance" or "Carryover Limit".
19. How do you configure service-based accrual that changes at 3, 5, and 10 years?
Fast formula: years = MONTHS_BETWEEN(TODAY(), HIRE)/12; IF years<3 THEN rate=8; ELSIF years<5 THEN rate=12; ELSE rate=16.
20. What happens if an accrual formula returns a NULL or zero?
No accrual is posted. The absence plan remains with current balance. Validation prevents unexpected zero accruals.
📋 3. Absence Plans & Eligibility (21–30)
21. How do you restrict an absence plan to only full-time employees?
Use eligibility criteria: plan enrollment condition: FTE = 1 via eligibility profile or Fast Formula (return Y/N).
22. What is the difference between "Enrolled" and "Assigned" status?
Assigned: employee is eligible based on enrollment criteria. Enrolled: after successful enrollment record creation (active participation).
23. Can an employee be enrolled in multiple absence plans simultaneously?
Yes, e.g., Vacation Plan + Sick Plan + Maternity Plan. Balances and rules are independent per plan.
24. What is a "Plan Year" and its significance?
Defines the 12-month period for accrual and carryover (Calendar year, Fiscal year, or Employee hire anniversary).
25. How do you configure a plan that does NOT allow carryover (use-it-or-lose-it)?
Set Maximum Carryover = 0. Any unused leave expires at plan year end; no balance rolls forward.
26. Explain "Cash-out" at termination vs annual cash-out.
Termination: pay unused leave via final settlement. Annual: employee can cash-out a portion (e.g., 5 days) each year, processed through payroll.
27. What is a "Wait Period" in absence plan?
Days after hire before employee can take leave (e.g., 90 days probation). Validation formula enforces waiting period.
28. How do you handle employees transferring between departments with different leave policies?
Use Plan Transfer rules: transfer remaining balance from old plan to new plan, mapped via balance transfer configuration.
29. What is a "Hardship Carryover"?
Exception rule allowing carryover beyond normal limit due to business reasons; requires HR approval override.
30. How does Absence Management support international locations (multiple countries)?
Create separate plans per country with local statutory rules, work schedules, and holiday calendars.
✅ 4. Validation & Approval Workflows (31–40)
31. Write a validation formula that blocks leave requests during probation period (first 90 days).
IF MONTHS_BETWEEN(REQUEST_DATE, ASG_HIRE_DATE)*30 < 90 THEN RETURN 'N'; ELSE RETURN 'Y';
32. What is a "Concurrent Leave" validation?
Prevents employee from taking two different paid leaves overlapping in same date range; enforced via validation formula checking existing absences.
33. How do you restrict maximum consecutive absence days (e.g., max 15 days)?
Validation formula using DURATION in days. IF duration > 15 THEN error message 'Exceeds max limit'.
34. Can absence approvals be delegated?
Yes, managers can delegate approval authority to another manager/HR using Delegation Management.
35. Explain multi-level approval routing (e.g., manager then HR director).
Configure BPM rule: if duration>10 days → first to Manager, then automatically to Director; else single approval.
36. What is a "Dynamic Approver" based on absence type?
Approver derived from absence attributes: Sick leave → Manager; Maternity → HRBP directly.
37. Can approval rules consider employee's location or job grade?
Yes, BPM rules can reference assignment attributes: Department, Grade, Location, Person Type.
38. What happens to pending approvals when a manager leaves the company?
Workflow reassigns to acting manager or HR; configurable via BPM reassignment rules.
39. How do you configure auto-approval for absences below 2 days?
BPM rule: IF duration <= 2 THEN auto-approve = TRUE, bypass manager approval.
40. What is "Retroactive Approval" and when is it used?
Approving absence after the start date. System recalculates balances and may trigger retro payroll for paid leave.
⚖️ 5. Balances, Carryovers & Initialization (41–50)
41. How do you initialize absence balances during implementation?
Use HCM Data Loader (HDL) with AbsenceBalance object or Spreadsheet Data Loader to upload opening balances per employee.
42. What is a "Balance Adjustment" and how is it performed?
Manual correction to employee leave balance (e.g., +5 days for goodwill). Done via "Adjust Balance" action in Absence admin.
43. How are carryover balances calculated at year-end?
Periodic batch "Process Carryover" computes remaining balance, applies max carryover limit, and rolls to new period.
44. Explain "Negative Balance" scenario after carryover.
If employee took more leave than accrued, year-end carryover may cause negative; system can reduce next year's accrual or require recovery.
45. Can employees view their leave balances in self-service?
Yes, Employee Self Service → Absence → Balances dashboard. Shows current, accrued, taken, and projected balances.
46. What is a "Balance Transfer" between plans?
Moving balance from one leave plan to another, e.g., when company changes vacation policy or employee transfers.
47. How does absence management handle leave encashment?
Encashment process creates payment element; reduces balance; transfers liability to payroll for payout.
48. What are "Projected Balances" and how are they useful?
Forecasted leave balance at future date considering planned absences and expected accruals; used for capacity planning.
49. Difference between "Available Balance" and "Plan Balance".
Available = Plan Balance - Pending approvals (not yet deducted). Plan Balance = actual accrued - taken.
50. What happens to balances when an employee terminates?
Final balance calculated; if policy allows payout, amount sent to payroll for final settlement; balance zeroed out.
💰 6. Payroll Integration (51–60)
51. How are paid absences reflected in payroll?
Absence plan linked to earnings element. When absence approved, element entry created with duration; payroll run includes that earnings.
52. How does unpaid leave deduction work in payroll?
Unpaid absence plan linked to deduction element. Payroll subtracts (daily salary × unpaid days) from normal salary.
53. Explain "Rate of Pay" formula for partial paid leaves (e.g., 70% sick pay).
Fast formula: return (ASG_SALARY/working_days) * 0.7. Defines how much employee gets per absence day.
54. What is the "Absence to Payroll" extract?
ETL process that transfers absence data (duration, pay rate) to create payroll elements. Scheduled periodic or real-time.
55. How does retroactive payroll work with absence? (e.g., backdated absence approval)
System triggers retro event. Payroll recalculates prior periods for affected employee, adjusting earnings/deductions.
56. Can absence deduction be overridden in payroll?
Yes, payroll administrator can manually adjust element entries before finalizing payroll run, but must maintain audit.
57. How are statutory sick pay (SSP) or workers' compensation integrated?
Absence plan calls SSP fast formula that determines eligibility and daily amount; payroll picks as third-party payment.
58. What happens if absence plan is not linked to any payroll element?
Absence is recorded but has no financial impact. Used for unpaid/unrecorded informational leaves.
59. How do you manage absence payments via third-party (e.g., insurance)?
Create third-party payment element. Absence plan triggers that element; payroll pays employee, and liability tracks reimbursement.
60. How to test payroll impact of an absence plan?
Run QuickPay for employee with approved absence; preview payroll results to validate earnings/deductions.
🏛️ 7. Statutory Leaves & Compliance (61–70)
61. How does Oracle support FMLA (Family & Medical Leave Act)?
Tracks 12-week entitlement per 12-month period, eligible conditions, intermittent leave, certification, and reporting.
62. What is an FMLA "Rolling Backward" method?
Entitlement calculated based on previous 12 months from request date. Oracle supports calendar, fixed, and rolling methods.
63. How do you track intermittent FMLA leaves?
Create absence pattern with recurring schedule (e.g., every Tuesday). System tracks cumulative hours toward total entitlement.
64. Explain how California Paid Sick Leave (PSL) is configured.
Accrual: 1 hour per 30 hours worked, 3 days annual cap, carryover allowed. Fast formula uses work hours from time card.
65. How does Oracle support parental leave (mandated by many states)?
Plan with 12 weeks, partial pay (e.g., 60% up to cap), certification (birth certificate). Integrates with state disability.
66. What is a "Statutory Absence Plan" vs "Company Plan"?
Statutory: required by law, uses legal entitlement rules. Company: additional employer-provided leave.
67. How to manage jury duty leave?
Plan with no accrual, unlimited duration, certification (court summons), pay type: court pay or company paid.
68. Bereavement leave – how configured?
Fixed days (3-5 days) per occurrence, no accrual, validation formula: requires death certificate for extended days.
69. How does ADA interact with absence management?
Reasonable accommodation: additional unpaid leave beyond FMLA. System tracks accommodation extensions separately.
70. What is the DOL WH-381 form and how does Oracle produce it?
FMLA designation notice. Oracle generates via HCM Extract or BI Publisher using FMLA tracking data.
📊 8. Reporting & OTBI (71–80)
71. Name 5 important OTBI subject areas for absence.
Absence Real Time, Absence Accrual Real Time, Absence Balance Real Time, Workforce Absence, Absence Certification Real Time.
72. How do you create a report showing employees nearing negative balance?
OTBI filter: "Available Balance" less than threshold (e.g., < 2 days), and planned absence duration greater than balance.
73. What is the "Absence Liability Report" used for?
Shows financial liability of unused paid leave (accrued balance × daily rate) for accounting accruals.
74. How to track absenteeism patterns (e.g., frequent Monday/Friday sick leaves)?
Use absence analysis dashboard or BI report grouping absence start day, employee, and absence type.
75. What is the "Absence Calendar" report for managers?
Visual calendar showing team members on leave per day, with color coding by absence type.
76. Can you schedule absence reports for automatic email delivery?
Yes, using Bursting or BI Publisher schedule with delivery to managers weekly.
77. What data does "Absence Inclusion" report provide?
Lists employees excluded from certain processes due to data issues (e.g., missing manager, enrollment errors).
78. How to monitor absence approval SLA (e.g., requests pending > 48 hours)?
OTBI report with filter: status = Pending, creation date older than 2 days.
79. What is "Absence Trend Analysis"?
Comparison of absence days by department, month, or year. Identifies seasonal spikes or department issues.
80. Can OTBI reports calculate absence rates (absence days / available working days)?
Yes, using calculated measures: sum(absence duration) / sum(scheduled working days) * 100 = absence percent.
🔧 9. Troubleshooting Common Issues (81–90)
81. Employee balance not accruing – what do you check?
Enrollment active? Plan start date? Accrual formula valid? Run "Calculate Absence Balances".
82. Absence request stuck in "Validation Failed".
Check validation formula: insufficient balance, probation active, max duration exceeded, or missing certification.
83. Error: "Approver not found" for absence request.
Manager missing on assignment, or manager lacks "Line Manager" role. Update manager assignment.
84. Payroll not picking up absence element entries.
1) Absence approved after payroll lock? 2) Plan not linked to element? 3) Run "Absence to Payroll" extract again.
85. Negative balance occurring despite hard limit rule.
Retroactive approval or backdated accrual change may cause. Run balance recalculation process.
86. Accrual shows zero for part-time employee.
FTE value = 0 in assignment. Update assignment FTE field to correct value (0.5 for half-time).
87. Error: "Invalid Absence Unit" (hours vs days mix).
Plan defined in days, request uses hours. Configure plan unit of measure and conversion factor.
88. How to debug accrual fast formula errors?
Enable formula trace, run "Test Formula", check compilation logs, review DBI values.
89. Manager not seeing team absence requests.
Person security profile missing department, or absence request not yet submitted (draft status).
90. Leave balance discrepancy between OTBI and employee dashboard.
Run balance reconciliation report. Pending approvals or effective date differences may cause gap.
🎯 10. Advanced & Scenario-Based (91–100)
91. Design a plan for unlimited PTO (no accrual, no carryover).
Disable accrual. Validation formula only checks manager approval; no balance tracking. Administrative tracking only.
92. How would you handle leave donation programs (employee donates days to another)?
Custom process: donor reduces balance via adjustment, recipient gets balance addition, with donation tracking records.
93. Employee took 5 unpaid days but payroll deducted 6 – why?
Work schedule: if unpaid includes a public holiday, system may exclude that day from deduction; check holiday calendar.
94. How to configure probationary employees cannot take leave first 30 days, after that max 5 days?
Validation formula: IF days_since_hire < 30 THEN error; ELSE IF duration > 5 THEN 'Needs Director Approval' END IF.
95. Convert hourly accrued leave to days for salaried employees.
Accrue in hours. Display as days using conversion: days = hours / standard_work_day_hours (e.g., 8).
96. What is "Absence Compensation Basis" and give example.
Defines if absence paid based on salary, hourly rate, or fixed amount. Example: Maternity leave at 100% for 8 weeks then fixed statutory amount.
97. How does Oracle manage absence during unpaid leave of absence (LOA) where no accrual happens?
Plan with accrual stop rule. No accrual posted during LOA. Balance remains frozen.
98. Explain "Absence Pattern with Rolling Entitlement".
E.g., FMLA: 12 weeks within any 12-month period calculated backward from each absence request.
99. How to calculate leave liability in multi-currency environment?
Liability report uses employee's salary currency; corporate conversion uses GL exchange rates.
100. What are the top 5 best practices for absence management implementation?
1) Clean historic balance data 2) Test accrual formulas with sample employees 3) Configure approval matrix early 4) Integrate with payroll from start 5) Train HR & managers on dashboard.
